NISMCAA Tutor Agreement

NISMCAA Tutor · tutor.nismcaa.com
Version 1.0 · Effective date: to be confirmed at launch
Based on: Standards and Procedures Handbook, Version 1.0

Before launch: This agreement is a plain-language draft and is not legal advice. A qualified lawyer must review it before it is used.

This agreement is between you (the "Tutor") and NISMCAA, the operator of NISMCAA Tutor ("NISMCAA," "we," "us"). By checking the box at sign-up, you agree to it. It works together with the NISMCAA Tutor Standards and Procedures Handbook (the "Handbook"), which is part of this agreement. If the two ever conflict, the Handbook's safeguarding rules always apply.

1. Your status

You are an independent contractor, not an employee of NISMCAA. You choose your rates, availability, and teaching methods within the Handbook's standards. You are responsible for your own taxes, and for any licenses or registrations your country requires.

2. Eligibility and checks

You confirm that you are 18 or older and that everything in your application is true. You agree to:

  • identity verification and a background check suited to your country before you teach, and every 12 months after that;
  • verification of the credentials and references you list; and
  • telling NISMCAA within 48 hours if you are arrested, charged, or convicted of any offense, or become the subject of any child-protection investigation.

3. Profile and rates

You will keep your profile honest and current, list only verified credentials, and include no contact details. You set rates in USD. NISMCAA keeps the platform fee set out on the Teach page. You will give existing clients 30 days' notice of a rate increase, and you will never charge more than your profile rate or ask for payment outside the platform.

4. Lessons

You will teach every lesson in the NISMCAA Meeting Room, on time, with your camera on, from a private, professional space, and post lesson notes within 24 hours. You will follow the cancellation, late-arrival, and no-show rules in the Handbook. In particular, if you are 15 minutes or more late, or do not show, the client gets a full refund.

5. Payment

Clients pay at booking, and Stripe holds the funds. After each lesson there is a 48-hour issue window. Lessons with no reported issue are cleared, and your earnings (the lesson price minus the platform fee set out on the Teach page) are paid weekly, on the payout day set out on the Teach page, to your verified Stripe account. Payouts for disputed lessons are held until the dispute is decided. You authorize NISMCAA to deduct refunds decided under the Handbook from your pending or future payouts.

6. Child safeguarding

You agree to:

  • confirm at the start of each lesson with a minor that an adult is present (under 13) or nearby (ages 13–17), and end the lesson politely if this rule is not met;
  • never contact a student or client outside the platform, message a minor directly, connect with a minor on social media, meet a student in person, or accept payment outside the platform;
  • NISMCAA recording lessons involving minors, kept for 90 days, and never making or sharing your own recordings without written consent given through the platform;
  • complete NISMCAA safeguarding training, and refresh it when asked; and
  • report any concern about a child's safety to NISMCAA the same day, and to emergency services right away if a child is in danger.

7. Conduct and enforcement

You will follow the Code of Conduct in the Handbook. NISMCAA may give reminders and warnings, suspend you, or permanently remove you under the Handbook's enforcement ladder. A credible safeguarding concern may lead to immediate suspension while we review it. After removal, pending payouts may be held to cover refunds and disputes.

8. Non-circumvention

For 12 months after you first meet a client through NISMCAA Tutor, you will not teach that client or their children outside the platform, or invite them to leave it. (Duration and enforceability to be confirmed by a lawyer.)

9. Privacy and content

You will protect the personal information of clients and students, use it only to teach on NISMCAA Tutor, and follow the Privacy section of the Handbook. You keep ownership of your original teaching materials, and you allow NISMCAA to display your profile content on the platform and in NISMCAA promotion of the platform.

10. Changes and ending

NISMCAA may update this agreement or the Handbook with at least 14 days' notice (urgent safety changes may take effect right away). Continuing to teach after the change means you accept it. Either party may end this agreement at any time. You will either complete or cancel (with full refunds) any lessons already booked.

11. Contact

Questions: info@nismcaa.com. Safety concerns: put "SAFETY" in the subject line.
