## Welcome

NISMCAA Tutor is the online music tutoring marketplace of NISMCAA, a US music education organization founded by John Adebowale and based in Atlanta, Georgia. We connect students of every age with music tutors we have vetted and approved. Our students live mostly in the United States, and also in Nigeria, the United Kingdom, Canada, and Ghana.

Many of our students are children, so everything in this handbook starts from one rule: **children's safety comes before everything else, including convenience, speed, and revenue.**

This handbook explains what each person can expect, and what each person agrees to:

- **Part A, Tutors:** how to join, teach, get paid, and stay in good standing.
- **Part B, Parents and Students:** how to book, pay, cancel, get help, and keep children safe.
- **Part C, NISMCAA Staff:** how we vet tutors, monitor quality, handle complaints, and escalate concerns, with response times.
- **Part D, Shared Policies:** payments, refunds and disputes, safeguarding, conduct, privacy, and accessibility. These apply to everyone.
- **Part E, At a Glance:** a one-page summary for each party.

Every tutor accepts the **Tutor Agreement** when signing up. Every parent or adult student accepts the **Client Agreement** when booking. Both agreements put this handbook into effect.

### Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Client** | The adult who books and pays. For a student under 18, this is always the parent or legal guardian. |
| **Student** | The person taking lessons. They may be the client (an adult) or a child of the client. |
| **Minor** | Anyone under 18. |
| **Meeting Room** | The NISMCAA video lesson room, built into the platform. |
| **Issue window** | The 48 hours after a lesson's scheduled end, when anyone can report a problem before the tutor is paid. |

# Part A: Standards for Tutors

## A1. Who can apply

To apply, you must:

- be at least 18 years old;
- live in a country where we can complete ID and background checks and pay you (at launch: the US, Nigeria, the UK, Canada, and Ghana);
- have a reliable internet connection, a working camera and microphone, and a quiet, private, professional teaching space;
- be able to teach in clear English (more languages may be added later); and
- show real skill and teaching experience in at least one instrument, voice, or music subject.

## A2. Application and approval steps

Each step must be completed before the next one starts. No one teaches until every step is done.

| Step | What happens | Who | Target time |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Apply | Submit the online application: instruments, levels, experience, credentials, two references, and a short teaching video (3–5 minutes). | Tutor | — |
| 2. Initial review | Staff check that the application is complete and meets the minimum standards. | Staff | 5 business days |
| 3. Identity verification | Photo of a government ID plus a live selfie check, through our identity provider. The name must match the name used for payouts. | Tutor + provider | Usually same day |
| 4. Background check | Criminal record and safeguarding check suited to your country (see A3). You give consent and pay nothing unless NISMCAA decides otherwise. | Tutor + provider | Varies by country |
| 5. Credential and reference check | Staff verify the degrees, exam certificates, and teaching qualifications you list, and contact both references. | Staff | 5 business days |
| 6. Interview and demo lesson | A 30-minute video call: a short conversation, then a 15-minute demo lesson taught to a staff member. | Tutor + staff | Scheduled within 10 business days |
| 7. Safeguarding training | Complete the NISMCAA child safeguarding module and pass the short quiz (80% or higher). | Tutor | Before approval |
| 8. Agreement | Accept the Tutor Agreement and this handbook. | Tutor | Before approval |
| 9. Profile review | Staff review the profile against the standards in A4. | Staff | 2 business days |
| 10. Approval | The profile goes live and you can accept bookings. | Staff | — |

**New tutor period:** For your first 90 days, or your first 10 lessons (whichever is longer), staff will look more closely at your reviews, cancellations, and any reports. Staff may sit in on one lesson, with the client's consent.

**If you are not approved:** We will tell you by email. If the reason was something you can fix, such as a missing document, you may reapply after 90 days. We do not share background check details with anyone except you and the check provider.

## A3. ID and background checks

- **Every tutor is checked.** Unlike some marketplaces, our background checks are never optional, and there is no "unchecked" tier.
- **Checks by country (to be confirmed with the chosen providers and a lawyer):**
  - **United States:** a consumer background check through an accredited provider, run with your written consent. It covers an SSN trace, national and county criminal records, and the national sex offender registry.
  - **United Kingdom:** an enhanced DBS check with the children's barred list where it is available for this role, otherwise a basic DBS check. Tutors may use the DBS Update Service.
  - **Canada:** a police information check that includes a vulnerable sector check.
  - **Nigeria:** a Police Character Certificate from the Nigeria Police Force, plus identity verification (for example, NIN).
  - **Ghana:** a Police Clearance Certificate from the Ghana Police Service CID, plus identity verification (for example, Ghana Card).
  - **Tutors who lived in another country within the last 5 years** may be asked for a check from that country too.
- **What disqualifies a tutor:** any offense against a child, any sexual offense, being on a sex offender registry or children's barred list, and any violent offense. We also look at fraud and serious dishonesty offenses, case by case, considering how serious it was and how long ago. Traffic offenses are not considered.
- **Renewal:** checks are renewed **every 12 months**. You have 30 days to complete a renewal. If a check lapses, your profile is hidden from search and no new bookings are allowed. Existing lessons with minors are paused until the check clears.
- **Duty to tell us:** you must tell NISMCAA within **48 hours** if you are arrested, charged, or convicted of any offense, or become the subject of any child-protection investigation.
- **Disputing a result:** you can dispute a result directly with the provider. We will pause, not close, your application while the dispute is open, for up to 30 days.

## A4. Profile standards

Your profile must be honest, current, and professional.

- **Name:** your real first name and last initial are shown publicly. Your full legal name is kept on file.
- **Photo:** a recent, clear photo of your face, well lit, with no filters, logos, or other people.
- **Credentials:** list only verified qualifications. A **Verified** badge appears next to each one staff have checked. Unverified claims are not allowed.
- **Instruments and levels:** list only what you are ready to teach today, for example "Piano: beginner to ABRSM Grade 5."
- **Ages taught:** state the age ranges you teach. Tutors who teach children under 13 must say so clearly.
- **No contact details anywhere on your profile:** no phone numbers, emails, social media handles, personal websites, or payment usernames. This includes text in your photos and videos.
- **Intro video (strongly recommended):** 1–2 minutes, filmed in your teaching space.
- **Availability:** keep your calendar up to date. Block out times you cannot teach.

## A5. Rates and the platform fee

- **You set your own rates** in US dollars (USD), per lesson length (for example 30, 45, or 60 minutes).
- **Minimum rate:** $[minimum] per 30 minutes (to be set by NISMCAA).
- **Platform fee:** NISMCAA keeps **[X]%** of each lesson price. It pays for vetting, background checks, the Meeting Room, payments, support, and safeguarding. Your dashboard shows the lesson price, the fee, and your earnings for every lesson before you accept it.
- **Trial lessons:** you may offer a discounted or free first lesson. The platform fee applies to paid trials only.
- **Packages:** you may offer packages (for example 4 or 8 lessons) at a discount. Package lessons follow the same cancellation and payout rules, one lesson at a time.
- **Changing your rates:** new rates apply to new clients right away. Existing clients keep their current rate for **30 days** after you announce a change, and lessons already booked keep the price paid.
- **Same price on and off the profile:** you may not charge one of our clients more than your profile rate, or ask for extra fees, tips, or materials payments outside the platform.

## A6. Teaching your lessons

- **Use the Meeting Room only.** All lessons happen in the NISMCAA Meeting Room, at the booked time.
- **Be ready 5 minutes early,** with your instrument tuned and your materials ready.
- **Camera on** for the whole lesson. Teach from a quiet, private, desk-based or studio setting. Bedrooms (unless set up as a studio), bathrooms, vehicles, and public places are not acceptable.
- **Dress and speak professionally.** No smoking, vaping, alcohol, or drugs during lessons.
- **For minors, check who is present** at the start of each lesson (see D5).
- **Lesson notes:** within **24 hours** of each lesson, post a short note in the platform: what you covered, what to practice, and what comes next.
- **Materials:** share sheet music and files only through the platform. Respect copyright. Use public-domain, licensed, or original materials.

## A7. Quality standards

To stay in good standing, tutors should keep:

| Measure | Standard |
|---|---|
| Average rating | 4.3 or higher (after 5 or more reviews) |
| Reply to booking requests and messages | Within 24 hours |
| Tutor cancellations (less than 24 hours before the lesson) | No more than 2 in any 90 days |
| Tutor no-shows | None. Each one is reviewed |
| Lesson notes posted | 90% or more of lessons |
| Background check | Always current |

If you fall below a standard, staff will first contact you to help, for example with coaching, a schedule review, or a technical check. Repeated or serious shortfalls move onto the ladder in D7.

## A8. How you get paid (summary)

See D1 for the full payment process. In short:

1. The client pays in full when they book. Stripe holds the money securely.
2. You teach the lesson.
3. The 48-hour issue window starts when the lesson ends.
4. If no issue is reported, the lesson is cleared for payout.
5. Payouts are sent **weekly, every [Friday]**, for all lessons cleared by 11:59 PM ET the Wednesday before.

You need a verified Stripe payout account in your legal name. You are an independent contractor, responsible for your own taxes. Stripe may ask for tax forms (for example a W-9 in the US, or a W-8BEN outside the US).

# Part B: Standards for Parents and Students

## B1. Accounts

- **Adults (18 and over)** can create their own account and book for themselves.
- **Students under 18** must be added under a **parent or guardian account**. The parent books, pays, receives all messages, and is responsible for the child's lessons. Children cannot open their own accounts.
- **Students aged 13–17** may have a linked student login to join lessons and see lesson notes. All messages are still copied to the parent.
- Keep your contact details current so we can reach you quickly.

## B2. Choosing a tutor

Every tutor on NISMCAA Tutor has passed ID verification, a background check, a credential and reference check, an interview, a demo lesson, and safeguarding training. Look at a tutor's profile, verified badges, reviews, intro video, ages taught, and availability. Message a tutor through the platform if you have questions before booking.

## B3. Booking

- Book from the tutor's calendar. Lessons can be booked at least **12 hours ahead** (a tutor may allow shorter notice) and up to 90 days ahead.
- **You pay the full price when you book.** Stripe holds the payment until after the lesson.
- The tutor confirms the booking within **24 hours**. If they do not, the booking is automatically canceled and you get a full refund.
- Your confirmation email shows the lesson time in your local time zone, the price, and the Meeting Room link.
- When booking for a child, you confirm that an adult will be present or nearby (see D5), and you agree to the recording rules (see D5.4).

## B4. Rescheduling and cancellation by the client

Every time window below is measured from the lesson's scheduled start time.

| When you act | Reschedule | Cancel |
|---|---|---|
| **24 hours or more** before the lesson | Free, from your dashboard | **Full refund** |
| **Between 24 and 4 hours** before the lesson | Only if the tutor agrees | **50% refund.** The tutor receives 50% for the reserved time |
| **Less than 4 hours** before, or after the start | Only if the tutor agrees | **No refund.** The tutor is paid |

- **Emergencies:** for a documented emergency (for example a medical emergency, bereavement, or a regional power or internet outage), contact us. Staff may refund or credit a late cancellation once per 6 months, at our discretion.
- **Tutors may be more generous.** A tutor can always choose to reschedule or refund a late cancellation.

## B5. Late arrivals and no-shows by the student

- **Student late:** the tutor waits for **15 minutes**. The lesson still ends at the scheduled time, and the full price applies.
- **Student no-show:** if the student has not joined within 15 minutes and has not contacted the tutor, the lesson is marked a student no-show. The tutor is paid. You may report a technical problem within the issue window and staff will review it.

## B6. When the tutor cancels, is late, or does not show

| What happens | What you get |
|---|---|
| Tutor cancels at any time | **Full refund** (or free reschedule, if you prefer) |
| Tutor joins **5–15 minutes** late | Your choice: a make-up of the lost minutes, or a refund for the lost time |
| Tutor joins more than **15 minutes** late, or not at all (tutor no-show) | **Full refund**, automatically once confirmed. You may also be offered a free make-up lesson |
| Lesson cut short by the tutor's technical failure | Make-up time or a refund for the lost portion |

## B7. Your role in quality and safety

- **For children:** stay present or nearby as described in D5. Make sure your child joins from a shared or open space, not a closed bedroom.
- **Keep everything on the platform:** messages, scheduling, payments, and lessons.
- **Leave honest reviews** (see D3).
- **Tell us right away** if anything worries you (see D5.6).

## B8. Our First Lesson Promise

If your first paid lesson with a new tutor is not a good fit, tell us within the **48-hour issue window**. We will give you a full refund for that lesson or a free first lesson with another tutor. This applies once per tutor. *(Final terms to be confirmed by John.)*

## C3. Tutor onboarding checklist

- [ ] Application complete; minimum standards met
- [ ] Identity verified; name matches payout account
- [ ] Background check cleared for each relevant country; date recorded; renewal date set (12 months)
- [ ] Each listed credential verified, or removed from the profile
- [ ] Two references contacted and recorded
- [ ] Interview and demo lesson completed; scorecard filed (musicianship, teaching clarity, warmth, professionalism, setting)
- [ ] Safeguarding module passed (80% or higher)
- [ ] Tutor Agreement accepted (version and date recorded)

- [ ] Profile reviewed: photo, no contact details, honest credentials, ages taught

## C4. Ongoing monitoring checklist

**Weekly**

- [ ] Review new reviews of 3 stars or lower, and every report
- [ ] Review tutor cancellations and no-shows; contact tutors who exceed the standards
- [ ] Check that payouts ran and that disputed lessons were held back
- [ ] Review automated flags for contact details or payment requests in messages

**Monthly**

- [ ] Spot-check at least 5 recorded lessons, prioritizing lessons with minors and new tutors
- [ ] Review tutors below the quality standards in A7
- [ ] List background checks due for renewal within 60 days and send reminders

**Quarterly**

- [ ] Report to John on complaints, refunds, safeguarding cases (anonymized), and tutor quality
- [ ] Review this handbook and suggest updates

## C5. Complaint and dispute procedure

1. **Log** the complaint in the case system with a case number, and **acknowledge** it within 1 business day.
2. **Freeze** the tutor's payout for that lesson if the report came within the issue window.
3. **Check for safeguarding.** If there is any child-safety element, pass the case to the Safeguarding Lead at once and follow C6 instead.
4. **Gather facts:** booking records, Meeting Room logs (join and leave times), messages, lesson notes, and the recording if available.
5. **Ask the other party** for their side. They have 3 business days to respond.
6. **Decide** within 7 business days of the report, using D2. Write the reason in plain language.
7. **Act:** release the payout, refund in full or in part, or credit. Record any conduct step on the ladder (D7).
8. **Tell both parties** the outcome and how to appeal.
9. **Close** the case and note lessons learned.

## C6. Safeguarding escalation procedure

1. **Immediate danger:** tell the reporter to call local emergency services (US and Canada 911, UK 999, Nigeria 112, Ghana 112). The Safeguarding Lead also contacts the authorities where appropriate.
2. **Suspend first, investigate second:** if a tutor is the subject of a credible concern involving a minor, suspend the tutor's account and hide their profile **at once**, pending review. Pause their payouts. Cancel and fully refund their upcoming lessons with minors.
3. **Preserve evidence:** lock the relevant recordings, messages, and logs so they are not deleted on schedule.
4. **Record** the facts as reported, using the reporter's own words where possible. Do not interview the child. Do not promise the reporter confidentiality you cannot keep.
5. **Refer** to the appropriate authorities (police and child protection services in the relevant country) when the law requires it or a child may be at risk. A lawyer should confirm the reporting duties in each country.
6. **Inform the parent** of the child concerned, unless the authorities advise otherwise.
7. **Decide:** after review, the Safeguarding Lead recommends reinstatement, a conduct step, or permanent removal. John approves permanent removals.
8. **Follow up:** where appropriate, tell other families of the removed tutor that the tutor no longer teaches on NISMCAA Tutor. Do this without sharing details.
9. **Keep** safeguarding records confidential, with access limited to the Safeguarding Lead, the deputy, and John.

## C7. Staff conduct

- Staff follow the same safeguarding rules as tutors: no private contact with students, and no off-platform contact with clients.
- Staff access personal data only when needed for a case, and never share it outside NISMCAA except with providers or authorities as described in D8.
- Staff must declare any personal connection to a tutor or client involved in a case, and step aside from it.

# Part D: Shared Policies

## D1. Payment model

1. **The client pays at booking.** The full lesson price is charged in USD through Stripe. The client's bank may add currency conversion fees.
2. **Stripe holds the money.** Neither the tutor nor NISMCAA uses the funds while the lesson is pending.
3. **The lesson happens** in the Meeting Room.
4. **The 48-hour issue window** opens when the lesson's scheduled end time passes. Either party can report a problem from the lesson page or by emailing info@nismcaa.com.
5. **No issue reported:** the lesson is cleared. The tutor's earnings (lesson price minus the **[X]%** platform fee) join the next weekly payout.
6. **Issue reported:** the payout for that lesson is held until the dispute is decided (D2).
7. **Weekly payouts** go out every **[Friday]** for lessons cleared by 11:59 PM ET the Wednesday before. Banks usually take 2–5 business days to deposit the money, depending on the country.
8. **No-shows by the tutor are refunded in full.** Student no-shows and late cancellations follow B4 and B5.

**Refund timing:** approved refunds go back to the original payment method within **2 business days** of the decision. Banks usually take 5–10 business days to show them.

**Never pay outside the platform.** No cash, bank transfers, mobile money, payment apps, or gift cards for lessons arranged through NISMCAA Tutor. Off-platform payments are not protected. Asking for or offering them breaks this handbook (see D7).

## D2. Refunds and disputes

### When refunds apply

| Situation | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Client cancels 24 hours or more ahead | Full refund |
| Client cancels 4–24 hours ahead | 50% refund |
| Client cancels less than 4 hours ahead, or student no-show | No refund (emergency exception: B4) |
| Tutor cancels, or tutor no-show | Full refund |
| Tutor 5–15 minutes late, or a technical failure on the tutor's side | Make-up time or a prorated refund |
| Lesson substantially not as described (wrong instrument or level, tutor unprepared, inappropriate setting) | Partial or full refund after review |
| First lesson with a new tutor not a good fit (First Lesson Promise) | Full refund or a free first lesson with another tutor |
| Safeguarding or conduct breach by the tutor | Full refund for the lesson; possibly more lessons refunded |

### How to raise a dispute

1. **Report** from the lesson page ("Report a problem") or email info@nismcaa.com with the lesson date, the tutor's name, and what happened. Report **within 48 hours** to stop the payout. Reports made up to **14 days** after a lesson are still reviewed. If the tutor has already been paid, a refund may then come as account credit.
2. **We acknowledge** your report within 1 business day.
3. **The other party responds** within 3 business days.
4. **We decide** within 7 business days of your report and explain why.
5. **Appeal:** either party may appeal once, within 5 business days of the decision, by replying to the decision email. A staff member who was not involved, or John, reviews it and decides within 5 business days. That decision is final on the platform.

Disputes over card charges should come to us first. Opening a chargeback with your bank before contacting us may pause your account while it is resolved.

## D3. Reviews and ratings

- **Only verified lessons can be reviewed:** one review per client per tutor every 30 days, within 14 days of a completed lesson.
- **Reviews of children's lessons are written by the parent.** Reviews must never include a child's full name, age, school, location, or photo.
- **Rating:** 1–5 stars, plus an optional written review.
- **Tutors may reply once,** politely and without sharing private details.
- **We remove reviews** that contain personal information, hate speech, threats, profanity, or advertising, that are about something other than the lesson, or that come from someone with a conflict of interest. We do not remove reviews simply because they are negative.
- **No buying or trading reviews,** and no offering discounts in exchange for good ones.
- **Tutors can rate client conduct privately.** Staff use those ratings to spot problems. They are not shown publicly.

## D4. Communication rules

- **All communication stays on the platform:** messages, scheduling, files, and lessons.
- **Tutors never message a minor directly.** Messages about a student under 18 go to the parent's account (a linked 13–17 student login sees copies, and the parent sees everything).
- **No sharing contact details** (phone, email, address, social media, messaging apps) between tutors and clients or students. The platform automatically flags shared contact details and payment requests for staff review.
- **No social media connections** between tutors and students under 18, ever, including after lessons end.

## D5. Child safeguarding

### D5.1 Our commitments

- Every tutor is ID-verified, background-checked every 12 months, and safeguarding-trained.
- Every lesson takes place in the NISMCAA Meeting Room. We do not offer in-person lessons.
- Every concern is taken seriously, acted on quickly, and recorded.

### D5.2 Adult present or nearby

- **Under 13:** a parent or trusted adult must be **in the same room or within sight and hearing** for the whole lesson. They should also be present for the first 5 minutes so the tutor can confirm the setup.
- **Ages 13–17:** a parent or trusted adult must be **at home and nearby**, able to check in at any time.
- **The child's room setup:** a shared or open space, or a room with the door open. Not in bed or a bathroom.
- **The tutor's role:** at the start of each lesson with a minor, the tutor confirms the student's identity on camera and that an adult is present or nearby. If no adult is available for a child under 13, the tutor **politely ends the lesson** and reports it. This counts as a student no-show, unless the parent fixes it within the 15-minute grace period.

### D5.3 Platform only

- Lessons happen **only** in the NISMCAA Meeting Room. No Zoom, WhatsApp, FaceTime, phone calls, or other apps, even "just this once." If the Meeting Room fails, the lesson is rescheduled through the platform.
- **No private contact** between tutors and students outside the platform, and **no off-platform payments** of any kind.
- **No in-person meetings** between tutors and students met through NISMCAA Tutor.
- **No gifts** from tutors to students. Clients may give small thank-you gifts through the platform only.

### D5.4 Recording and consent

- **NISMCAA records lessons involving a student under 18** for safety and quality. *(John to confirm whether to record all lessons.)* Parents consent to this when booking. Tutors consent in the Tutor Agreement.
- Recordings are encrypted, stored on NISMCAA systems, kept for **90 days**, and then deleted automatically. They are kept longer only when needed for an open safety case or dispute, or when the law requires it.
- **Who can view recordings:** authorized staff for a safety case, dispute, or quality check; the parent of the child in the lesson, on request; and authorities when the law requires it.
- **Tutors and clients may not make their own recordings, screenshots, or photos** of a lesson without written consent from the other party (and from the parent, for a minor) given through the platform. Recordings may never be shared publicly or posted on social media.
- **Marketing use** of any lesson image or recording requires separate, written, opt-in consent from the parent (for a minor) or the adult student, and that consent can be withdrawn at any time.

### D5.5 Tutor safeguarding duties

- Keep every interaction professional, encouraging, and age-appropriate.
- Never ask a student to keep a secret, turn off their camera so the parent cannot see, or change rooms.
- Never comment on a student's appearance or body, other than posture and technique relevant to playing.
- Ask the student to adjust their own posture, hand position, or instrument. Never ask them to show parts of their body beyond what teaching requires.
- If a child tells you something worrying, listen calmly, do not promise to keep it secret, do not investigate, and **report it to NISMCAA the same day** (or right away if the child is in danger).

### D5.6 How to report a concern
Anyone (a parent, student, tutor, or staff member) can report.

1. **If a child is in immediate danger,** call local emergency services first: **US and Canada 911 · UK 999 · Nigeria 112 · Ghana 112.**
2. **Use "Report a safety concern"** in the Meeting Room or on any lesson or profile page, or email **info@nismcaa.com** with **"SAFETY"** in the subject line.
3. **Say what happened,** when it happened, who was involved, and anything you saw or heard. Do not wait for proof.
4. **What happens next:** the Safeguarding Lead responds within 2 hours for urgent reports (see C2). The tutor may be suspended while we look into it. We keep you informed as far as we can without risking the child or the investigation.
5. **No retaliation:** no one will be penalized for reporting a concern in good faith.

## D6. Code of conduct

**Everyone agrees to:**

- treat others with respect, patience, and kindness;
- be honest about identity, qualifications, and lesson details;
- keep lessons, messages, and payments on the platform;
- respect privacy, copyright, and time zones;
- follow the safeguarding rules in D5; and
- report problems instead of handling them privately.

**Not allowed:**

- harassment, bullying, discrimination, hate speech, or threats;
- sexual or romantic remarks or contact of any kind with a student;
- sharing contact details, or taking contact or payment off the platform;
- fake reviews, fake accounts, or account sharing;
- teaching or attending while impaired by alcohol or drugs;
- recording without consent, or sharing anyone's personal information; and
- poaching clients away from the platform.

**Zero tolerance (immediate suspension, likely permanent removal, and possible report to authorities):**

- any sexual content, grooming behavior, or abuse involving a minor;
- deliberately avoiding parent oversight, or arranging secret contact with a minor;
- lying about identity, or a background check result that disqualifies you; and
- threats of violence or fraud.

## D7. Enforcement ladder

| Level | Step | Typical triggers | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Friendly reminder** | First minor slip: late lesson notes, a missed reply window, one late cancellation | Written note and guidance. Logged |
| **2** | **Formal written warning** | Repeated minor issues, falling below the quality standards, first attempt to share contact details | Warning with a fix-it plan and deadline. Profile may be lowered in search |
| **3** | **Suspension (7–30 days)** | Breaking a written warning, a tutor no-show pattern, off-platform payment requests, serious rudeness | Account paused; no new bookings; upcoming lessons canceled and refunded; return only after a review call |
| **4** | **Permanent removal** | Zero-tolerance breach, repeated suspensions, fraud | Account closed; pending payouts held for refunds and disputes; may not rejoin |

- Staff may skip levels when the situation calls for it. **Any safeguarding concern can lead straight to suspension.**
- Level 1 and 2 records expire after **12 months** without further problems. Safeguarding records never expire.
- The same ladder applies to clients and students, for example abusive behavior, repeated no-shows, or chargeback abuse.
- Every step above Level 1 can be appealed once (see D2, step 5).

## D8. Privacy and data

- **What we collect:** account and contact details, booking and payment records (card details are handled by Stripe, not stored by us), messages, lesson notes, recordings (D5.4), and, for tutors, identity and background check results.
- **Background checks:** the provider holds the full report. NISMCAA keeps only the result (cleared or not cleared), the date, and the renewal date.
- **Children's data:** we collect as little as possible about children: first name, age range, instrument, and level. Parents control it. We never sell it or use it for advertising.
- **We never sell personal data.** We share it only with service providers who help run the platform (payments, identity checks, background checks, video, email), or when the law requires it.
- **How long we keep data:** recordings for 90 days (D5.4). Messages and lesson records for the life of the account, plus a period required for tax and legal purposes (to be confirmed by a lawyer). Safeguarding records for as long as the law requires.
- **Your rights:** you may ask to see, correct, download, or delete your data by emailing info@nismcaa.com. We reply within 30 days. Some records must be kept for legal, tax, or safety reasons.
- **Laws:** NISMCAA intends to follow the privacy and children's privacy laws that apply in each country we serve. In the US these include COPPA for children under 13; others include the UK GDPR and Age Appropriate Design Code, Canada's PIPEDA, Nigeria's Data Protection Act, and Ghana's Data Protection Act. A full Privacy Policy, reviewed by a lawyer, will be published separately.
- **Security:** two-factor sign-in is available (and required for tutors and staff), data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and staff access is limited by role.

## D9. Accessibility

- We aim to meet **WCAG 2.2 Level AA** across tutor.nismcaa.com and the Meeting Room: keyboard navigation, screen reader support, captions where available, good color contrast, and resizable text.
- **Accommodations:** students may note accessibility needs on their profile (for example extra processing time, visual or hearing needs, or neurodivergent learning styles). The tutor sees these before the first lesson.
- Tutors may list experience teaching students with disabilities. This is self-reported unless verified.
- To request help or report an accessibility barrier, email **info@nismcaa.com** with "Accessibility" in the subject. We reply within 2 business days.

## D10. Changes to this handbook

We may update this handbook. We will give at least **14 days' notice** by email and on the site before material changes take effect, except for urgent safety changes, which may take effect right away. The version number and effective date at the top show the current version.
