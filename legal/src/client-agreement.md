# NISMCAA Client Agreement (Parents and Students)

**NISMCAA Tutor** · tutor.nismcaa.com  
Version 1.0 · Effective date: January 1, 2027 (placeholder, to be confirmed at launch)  
Based on: Standards and Procedures Handbook, Version 1.0

> **Before launch:** This agreement is a plain-language draft and is not legal advice. A qualified lawyer must review it before it is used.

This agreement is between you (the "Client": an adult student, or the parent or legal guardian of a student under 18) and NISMCAA, the operator of NISMCAA Tutor ("NISMCAA," "we," "us"). By checking the box when you book, you agree to it. It works together with the **NISMCAA Tutor Standards and Procedures Handbook** (the "Handbook"), which is part of this agreement.

## 1. Your account
You confirm that you are 18 or older. If you are booking for a child, you confirm that you are their parent or legal guardian and that you are responsible for their lessons and their conduct. Children may not hold their own accounts.

## 2. Booking and payment
You pay the full lesson price in USD when you book. Stripe holds the payment until after the lesson. Your bank may add currency conversion fees. You will pay only through the platform, never directly to a tutor.

## 3. Cancellations, late arrivals, and no-shows
Every time window is measured from the lesson's scheduled start time.

- **24 hours or more before:** free reschedule or **full refund**.
- **4–24 hours before:** **50% refund**, or a reschedule if the tutor agrees.
- **Less than 4 hours before, or a student no-show** (not joined within 15 minutes): **no refund**. NISMCAA may make one emergency exception every 6 months, at its discretion.
- **The tutor cancels, is 15 minutes or more late, or does not show:** **full refund**.
- **The tutor is 5–15 minutes late, or has a technical failure:** make-up time or a prorated refund.

## 4. Problems, refunds, and disputes
Report a problem within the **48-hour issue window** after the lesson to hold the tutor's payment. Reports made up to 14 days after a lesson are still reviewed. We acknowledge reports within 1 business day and decide within 7 business days. You may appeal once, within 5 business days of the decision. You agree to contact us before opening a chargeback with your bank. Approved refunds go back to your original payment method.

## 5. First Lesson Promise
If your first paid lesson with a new tutor is not a good fit, and you tell us within 48 hours, you will receive a full refund for that lesson or a free first lesson with another tutor (once per tutor).

## 6. Child safety
If the student is under 18, you agree that:

- an adult will be **in the room or within sight and hearing (under 13)**, or **at home and nearby (ages 13–17)**, for every lesson;
- your child will join from a shared or open space, using only the NISMCAA Meeting Room;
- you will not share contact details or social media with the tutor, arrange contact or meetings outside the platform, or pay a tutor directly;
- NISMCAA may **record lessons** involving your child for safety and quality, keep the recordings for 90 days, and then delete them. You will not make your own recordings of other people without their consent; and
- you will report any concern right away, using "Report a safety concern" or by emailing info@nismcaa.com with "SAFETY" in the subject (and calling emergency services if a child is in danger).

## 7. Conduct and reviews
You and your student will treat tutors with respect and follow the Code of Conduct in the Handbook. Reviews must be honest and about the lesson, and must never include a child's personal details. NISMCAA may give warnings, or suspend or close accounts, for abusive behavior, repeated no-shows, off-platform payments, or misuse of chargebacks.

## 8. Privacy
We collect only what we need to run lessons, we collect as little as possible about children, and we never sell personal data. You may ask to see, correct, or delete your data at info@nismcaa.com. Full details are in the Privacy section of the Handbook and in the NISMCAA Privacy Policy.

## 9. What NISMCAA does and does not do
NISMCAA vets tutors, provides the platform, and handles payments and disputes as described in the Handbook. Tutors are independent contractors. NISMCAA does not guarantee particular musical results, exam grades, or outcomes.

## 10. Changes
We may update this agreement or the Handbook with at least 14 days' notice (urgent safety changes may take effect right away). Booking after a change takes effect means you accept it.

## 11. Contact
Questions: **info@nismcaa.com**. Safety concerns: put **"SAFETY"** in the subject line.
